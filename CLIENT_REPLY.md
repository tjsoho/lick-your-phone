All of the points from your last note are done and live. Here is what changed.

**Pricing per week.** Every subscription is now shown per week, on the service
slides, in the price bar at the top, on Your Summary and in the Investment
Summary. Videography Subscription and Community Management stay per month, as
you asked. One-off services no longer say per month or per week. They read
"one-off payment".

While we were in there we found something worth flagging. One-off services
were being added into the monthly running total at the top, so a $2,495
photography shoot was reading as $2,495 a month. One-off payments now sit on
their own, separate from the weekly figure, and are never folded into it.

**Weekly Total.** "Monthly Total" is now "Weekly Total". We have kept the
checkout to the weekly figure only, so there is no monthly line underneath it.
Say the word if you would still like the monthly amount shown there and we
will put it back. The monthly figure is on the contract either way, which is
what the client is billed against.

**You save.** Bigger and bolder. It is now the second largest figure on the
card, after the total itself.

**Offer ends.** The countdown now sits inside the Investment Summary, just
above the Proceed button, as well as in the strip along the top. It disappears
once the proposal is signed.

**Adding a service.** Every service is added the same way now. A service with
a single price shows one price box with a tick in the corner, exactly like the
term boxes on Annually, Six months and Three months. The price sits inside the
box and the whole box is clickable. The "I want this" switch is gone.
Complimentary services work the same way, with the word "Complimentary" where
the price would be.

**Payment page.** The line asking them to authorise the payment is gone. The
Save button stays greyed out until the box is ticked, so there is nothing left
to nag them about. Everything else still speaks up as before, such as a
missing account name or a short BSB.

We also corrected the note at the bottom of that page. It still mentioned card
details being held by a payment provider, which was left over from when a card
was an option. We only collect bank details, and they never leave our own
server, so the note now says that.

**Onboarding.** The Primary Contact shortcut reads "Same as above". Ticking it
still copies the director's first name, last name and email down.

**All Done page.** The "Yours to keep" section is centred. It was built for
three cards, and now that the terms sit inside the agreement PDF there are
two, so the block sizes itself to whatever is in it.

**Your copies by email.** When a client finishes onboarding, their signed
agreement and their onboarding answers are now passed straight through to the
automation that sends their email, so both can go out with it. They are the
same two files the All Done screen offers, so the email and the screen can
never give out different versions.
