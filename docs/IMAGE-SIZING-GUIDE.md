# Image sizing guide for Movement Assembly

Use these dimensions when creating or uploading images so they look sharp and load efficiently across the site.

---

## Landscape images

| Use | Ideal size (px) | Aspect ratio | Notes |
|-----|-----------------|-------------|--------|
| **Hero background** (home) | **1920 × 1080** or **2400 × 1350** | 16:9 | Full-width; 2400px for retina. |
| **Section backgrounds** (e.g. Section 3, 5, 7) | **1920 × 1080** | 16:9 | Full-bleed section images. |
| **Blog / article cover** | **1200 × 630** or **1200 × 628** | ~1.91:1 | Also good for social sharing (OG). |
| **Blog cards & team cards** | **1200 × 675** or **1600 × 900** | 16:9 | Card thumbnails. |
| **Contact / About hero** | **1920 × 1080** | 16:9 | Same as hero background. |
| **General “wide” images** | **1920 × 1080** | 16:9 | Safe default for any landscape. |

**Landscape summary:** **1920 × 1080** is the standard. Use **2400 × 1350** for hero if you want extra sharpness on large screens.

---

## Portrait images

| Use | Ideal size (px) | Aspect ratio | Notes |
|-----|-----------------|-------------|--------|
| **Hero carousel cards** (home) | **1080 × 1320** | 9:11 | Matches carousel card crop. |
| **Team / profile photos** | **800 × 800** (square) or **600 × 800** | 1:1 or 3:4 | Headshots and profile imagery. |
| **General “tall” images** | **1080 × 1350** | 4:5 | Good default for portrait content. |

**Portrait summary:** **1080 × 1320** (9:11) for hero carousel; **800 × 800** for profile/square.

---

## Icons & small graphics

| Use | Ideal size (px) | Notes |
|-----|-----------------|--------|
| **Service/approach icons** | **400 × 400** (square) | Icons next to text; SVG or PNG. |
| **Small icons / UI** | **200 × 200** | Logos, badges, small graphics. |

---

## Quick reference

- **Landscape:** **1920 × 1080** (or 2400 × 1350 for hero).
- **Portrait:** **1080 × 1320** (hero carousel) or **800 × 800** (profiles).
- **Format:** JPEG or WebP for photos; PNG or SVG for icons/graphics.
- **File size:** Keep under ~300–500 KB per image where possible for fast loading.
